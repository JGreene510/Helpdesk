HOW TO GET COMPUTER HELP
========================

Print this page or share it with everyone who needs computer help.

The Helpdesk website is:

     http://______________________:3000

(Open it in any web browser, on any computer or phone that is on our
network. You do not need a password to ask for help.)


--------------------------------------------------------------------
TO ASK FOR HELP
--------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Open the Helpdesk website.

2. Under "Submit a Support Ticket", fill in the boxes:

     Your Name ........ your full name
     Email Address .... your email. We use it to write back to you, and
                        you will need it to check on your ticket.
     Category ......... pick the closest one:
                          Software = a program or app is not working
                          Hardware = something you can touch (printer,
                                     screen, mouse, laptop)
                          Network  = internet, Wi-Fi, can't reach files
                          Other    = not sure
     Priority ......... how much is it hurting?
                          Low    = a general question
                          Medium = it is slowing my work down
                          High   = I cannot work at all
                        (Please use High only when it is true. It
                        helps us help the right people first.)
     Subject .......... a few words, like "Printer jammed"
     Description ...... tell the story:
                          - What were you trying to do?
                          - What happened instead?
                          - Did you see an error message? Type it
                            exactly, or write what it said.
                          - What have you already tried?

3. Click "Submit Ticket".

4. A green message appears with your ticket number, like HD-001.
   WRITE THAT NUMBER DOWN. You need it to check on your ticket.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
TO CHECK ON YOUR TICKET
--------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Open the Helpdesk website.
2. Find the box "Track an existing ticket".
3. Type your ticket number (like HD-001) and the SAME email address
   you used when you asked for help.
4. Click "Track".

You will see one of these:

     open ....... we got your request, nobody has started yet
     assigned ... someone is working on it
     resolved ... it is done. Read the "Resolution" note to see what
                  we did.

If it says "No ticket found", check the number and make sure the email
is exactly the same one you used before.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
HELPFUL TIPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------

* One problem per ticket. If you have two problems, send two tickets.

* NEVER put a password in a ticket. We will never ask you for one.

* Other people cannot look up your ticket without BOTH your ticket
  number and your email address, so keep the number to yourself.

* If you send several tickets in a row, the website may ask you to
  wait a few minutes. That is normal. Try again shortly.

* If it is urgent and you cannot work at all, also tell us in person
  or by phone:
